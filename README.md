# GoSG Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 October 2026_

GoSG ("the app") helps you get around Singapore by public transport. This policy explains what
data the app uses. GoSG has no account system and no server of its own.

## Data that stays on your device
- **Saved stops, alerts and settings** are stored locally on your phone.
- **Your location** (approximate or precise) is used only while the app is open, to show nearby
  stops, stations and facilities and your position on the map. It is not stored or shared with us.

## Data sent to others
- **LTA DataMall (Land Transport Authority, Singapore).** The app requests bus arrivals, routes,
  train status and map data from LTA. For the bicycle parking layer, the map position you are
  viewing is sent to LTA to find parking nearby.
- **Google AdMob (advertising).** The app shows ads. Google may collect your device's advertising
  ID, IP address and device and usage information to serve and measure ads. In regions where the
  law requires it, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice later under
  "Ad privacy settings" on the Go ad-free screen. See
  [Google's policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
- **Firebase Analytics (Google).** The app records anonymous usage events, such as which screens
  are opened and when an alert is set. It does not record your location, search text or any
  personal data.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google).** To send service disruption notifications, the app
  subscribes your device to a topic. Google processes a device token for this.
- **Google Play.** Purchases ("Remove ads") are handled by Google Play. We do not see your payment
  details.

## Notifications
The app asks permission to show notifications for bus arrival alerts and disruption notices. You
can turn this off in your phone's settings. Arrival alerts run for 30 minutes, then stop.

## Children
GoSG is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

## Your choices
- Deny or revoke location and notification permissions in your phone's settings.
- Reset your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.
- Remove ads with the one-time purchase.
- Uninstall the app to delete all data it stores locally.

## Changes
We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
